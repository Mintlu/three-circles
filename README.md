# Three Circles 🌱

> Discover what you love, what you're good at, and what matters — then try it in the real world.

## Why Three Circles?

Many of us ask:

**What should I do with my life?**

Three Circles explores a different way to find out.

Instead of asking AI to tell you your purpose, it helps you explore the intersection of:

❤️ **What you love**  
⭐ **What you're good at**  
🌍 **What matters to you**

And then turns that intersection into something small you can actually try.

## How it works

### 1. Discover

Reflect on three simple questions:

- What do I love doing?
- What am I good at?
- What problems or needs do I care about?

### 2. Experiment

Three Circles turns your answers into small real-world experiments.

For example:

> I love drawing.  
> I'm good at explaining things.  
> I care about children enjoying learning.

Instead of saying:

> "You should become a teacher."

Three Circles might suggest:

> **Create a simple drawing game that teaches one idea to a child. Try it with three children this week and see what happens.**

### 3. Reflect

After each experiment, ask:

- Did I enjoy it?
- Was I good at it?
- Did it feel meaningful?
- Would I like to do it again?

Over time, patterns begin to appear.

## Our Principle

**AI should not decide your life's purpose.**

AI can help you notice patterns, ask better questions, and design experiments.

But the answer should come from living, trying, creating, helping, failing, learning, and trying again.

## Vision

We imagine a world where more people spend their time doing things they:

❤️ love,  
⭐ can become good at,  
🌍 and believe are worth doing.

If more people can find that intersection and use it to contribute to the people and communities around them, perhaps we can make the world a little more peaceful and a little better.

## Status

> Help one person discover one meaningful thing worth trying this week.
